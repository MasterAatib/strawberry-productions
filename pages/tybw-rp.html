Bleach: Thousand Year Blood War – Resource Pack

TYBW-RP is a UI-focused resource pack inspired by the Bleach: Thousand Year Blood War anime. It replaces the default Minecraft interface with a darker, sharper, and more atmospheric design that fits the Quincy vs. Soul Reaper aesthetic.

The pack currently focuses on menu backgrounds, buttons, icons, and HUD elements. Whether you're building in the Seireitei or exploring Hueco Mundo, TYBW-RP keeps the Bleach mood alive while you play.
