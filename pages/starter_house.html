Description

Starter House gives you a ready‑to‑use survival base right when you spawn. No more punching trees for your first night — just walk in, claim your bed, and start exploring.

The house includes everything a beginner needs: a full set of basic tools, a furnace, a crafting table, storage chests with essential resources, a small farm, and even a hidden treasure room. Perfect for new players or anyone who wants to skip the early grind.
